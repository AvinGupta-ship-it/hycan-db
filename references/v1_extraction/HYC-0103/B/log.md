## Sources extracted from

- Table 1 (main p. 11316, page image main-3): H2 uptake at -196 C and 20 bar (footnote f) and at 1 bar (values in parentheses, footnote g) for all 10 samples, 20 values, table_direct. Also the characterization on every row: surface area (BET), micropore surface area (parentheses, t-plot), total pore volume, micropore volume (parentheses, t-plot), DFT pore size maxima (notes only).
- Figure 3 (main p. 11317, page image main-4): six 77 K H2 isotherms, 0-20 bar: (a) ZTC3, (b) ZTC2, (c) ZTC1 (filled symbols), (d) Ac-ZTC3, (e) Ac-ZTC2, (f) Ac-ZTC1 (open symbols). All separable markers digitized (see below). The Table 1 20-bar value of each of these six samples is the same measurement as the highest-pressure plotted marker (digitized at 19.91-20.01 bar, within 0.4 bar) and is recorded once, as the table row with the digitized point in notes.
- Section 2.1 (p. 11315): synthesis of ZTC1-ZTC5 (zeolite Y CBV720 template, acetonitrile CVD at 800/850/900 C, 900 C heat treatment, HF/HCl template removal) and KOH activation (1:4, 800 C, 1 h, N2) giving Ac-ZTC1-5.
- Section 2.2 (p. 11315): N2 sorption at -196 C (Micromeritics ASAP 2020), BET P/P0 0.06-0.22, total pore volume at P/P0 ~0.99, t-plot micropore area and volume, NLDFT PSD.
- Section 2.3 (p. 11315): H2 99.9999% (molecular sieve filter), 0-20 bar, Hiden IGA-003 gravimetric analyzer (microbalance resolution +/-0.2 ug), degassing to 10^-7 bar at 120 C overnight, measurement at -196 C in a liquid nitrogen bath, buoyancy correction with carbon density 1.5 g/cm3 and hydrogen density 0.04 g/cm3. Basis (excess/absolute/total) never named.
- p. 11317 text: assumed packing densities 0.8 g/cm3 (ZTC) and 0.6 g/cm3 (Ac-ZTC), refs 22a,32,33 (stored in packing_density_g_cm3 with a note that they are assumed).
