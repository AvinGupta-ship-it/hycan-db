## Digitization acceptance rule and results
- A tool marker is used only if its pixel count is 0.6-1.3 times that of the same series' legend marker (an unoccluded marker at the same tolerance). Smaller groups are partly hidden behind other series or white-filled open markers (centroid biased); larger ones are merged markers. Rows with count below 0.8 times the legend count carry extraction_confidence 2 and a note.
- Legend markers, the panel letters "(C)" and "(F)" and legend text (black series) are excluded by position and listed in the archives.
- `check` against stated values passed (tolerance 5%): Fig. 2C a-700 at 10 and 30 bar, a-800 at 10 and 30, a-900-1 at 10, a-1000 at 10 and 30, a-900-2 at 10 and 30, a-900-3 at 10 and 30, a-900-4 at 30 (all within 1.3%); Fig. 2F a-700 at 5 bar (0.44%), a-900-1 at 7.64 (0.95%), a-1000 at 7.72 (1.62%), a-900-4 at 12.83 (2.73%); Fig. 12 at 50 bar (0.62%). Earlier checks of a-900-1 at 30 bar and a-900-4 at 10 bar failed because no marker of the archive lay near those pressures (interpolation across a gap); the archives were regenerated unchanged and checked only where a marker exists. Fig. 2F archives a-800, a-900-2, a-900-3 have no stated value to check against and remain status unchecked; they share the checked calibration.
- Marker counts do not match what is on the plot, and `isotherm_point_count` is left empty on every row. The reasons:
  - Open (desorption, d-) markers of all 14 curves in Fig. 2C/2F and of Fig. 12 were not digitized. They are drawn as pale 1-pixel outlines at about 5 px; colour isolation breaks each outline into 2-16 pixel fragments (tested on d-700-1 in Fig. 2C at tolerances 60-100), so no marker centre could be separated. About 9-10 open markers per curve are visible at 8-50 bar.
  - Below about 5 bar all series pile onto each other in both panels; only markers that passed the size rule were taken. Visible filled markers per series at 8-50 bar are about 9; separated per series: C 700: 9, 800: 8, 900-1: 5, 1000: 11, 900-2: 11, 900-3: 8, 900-4: 10; F 700: 7, 800: 1, 900-1: 2, 1000: 11, 900-2: 10, 900-3: 8, 900-4: 10 (including points merged into Table 6 / text rows: C 8, F 1).
  - In Fig. 2F the filled a-800-1 and a-900-1 markers sit behind a-1000-1 and open markers and are almost entirely hidden; in Fig. 2C a-900-1 is mostly hidden behind a-1000-1 and a-800-1.
- The filled (a-) markers in Fig. 2C and 2F end at about 45 bar; the only markers at 50 bar are open ones. Fig. 14 (a replot of the absolute data with fit curves) shows filled markers reaching 50 bar.

## Coding decisions
- Basis: the paper names absolute (Fig. 2C, Table 6) and excess (Fig. 2F, Fig. 11 maximum) uptake, defines the absolute value by Eq. (3) from the excess value (Eq. 1 with buoyancy term), and reports both side by side: basis_stated = true, quote in basis_evidence. Fig. 12 and Table 8 values have no stated basis (gravimetric, buoyancy correction in Eq. 1, so basis_inferred = excess by the instrumental default).
- Excess and absolute curves of one sample come from one run but are separate plotted curves; they have separate isotherm_ids (I1-I7 absolute, I8-I14 excess, I15 Fig. 12).
- wt% convention: not defined, no paired units: unknown / none.
- Temperature printed as -196 C: stored 77.15 K. Room temperature value printed as 25 C: 298.15 K.
- Instrument: "PSD-PH device" with a magnetic-levitation ("maglev") balance, no manufacturer or model: instrument_identified = false; buoyancy correction in Eq. (1): calibration_described = true. "Repeated experiments under most working conditions" with no results: replicates_reported = false.
- Surface area: BET by N2 at -196 C on each final sample, range and consistency criteria not stated. Degassing for H2: 300 C, 120 min, vacuum (end pressure not stated). Sample mass for the H2 test and H2 purity not stated. average_pore_diameter_nm = d1 (4V/A by BET, adsorption); d2 (BJH desorption) in notes.
