## Marker counts and markers not extracted
- Figure 8 and 1S: markers every 0.5 bar from 2.5 to 19 bar (34) plus the end marker (35) on every curve. Below about 2 bar the markers of all curves overlap heavily (blob sizes 5-30 times a single marker) and cannot be separated, so no point below 2.5 bar is extracted except where a marker is isolated (Fig 8 curve d at 1.48 bar and Figure 1S curves d and g at 1.0-1.5 bar are isolated and kept). `isotherm_point_count` = 35 is therefore a lower bound; markers below 2 bar are not counted.
- Merged (not separable, not extracted): Fig 8 curves a and b from 9 to 19 bar (21 grid positions each) and end marker (merged blob 2.009 wt% at 19.94 bar); curves h and i from 2.5 to 14.5 bar (23 each); curves e and f at 2.5 and 3.5 bar. Figure 1S: a and b from 9.5 to 19 bar (20 each); h and i from 2.5 to 12.5 bar (21 each); e and f at 2.5 and 3.5 bar.
- Identity of the two overlapping CYE curves a (CYE750) and b (CYE550) below 9 bar: assigned by ordering (b above a), assuming they do not cross; the paper letters the curves in order of 20 bar uptake and both end at 2.0 wt%. Treat as uncertain.
- Marker symbols alternate between up and down triangles on the blue curves; the paper does not say these are adsorption and desorption, so all rows are coded adsorption (paper: "reversible, no hysteresis"). Same for Figure 10.
- Figure 10: markers resolved, merged blobs and the triangle at p = 0 of curve b were not all separable. Panel A curve b: 17 resolved markers, unresolved blobs near 0.02, 0.26, 0.39, 1.07 and 1.14 bar, an up-triangle at about 0 bar left out as unassigned (count 22).
