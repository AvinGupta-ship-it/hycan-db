## Digitization method and deviations

- All three plots are greyscale with every series in black, distinguished only by marker shape; the colour isolation of digitize_figure.py cannot separate series. For each series I identified every marker (pixel inspection plus a template fit of the four marker shapes to each overlapping cluster), built a masked copy of the page image in the work directory that keeps the original pixels only inside that series' marker footprints (template shape dilated by 1 px; a 1 px gap where two markers of the same image touch), and ran `digitize_figure.py extract --mode markers --open-px 0 --tolerance 230` on it. Marker list: work/markers_fig5.json; masked images: work/masks5/ and work/masks67/.
- Figure 5 is archived per branch (Fig5_<series>_ads.json, Fig5_<series>_des.json) instead of one file per legend series, because the adsorption and desorption markers of a series often overlap by 0-2 px and would merge in one image. One extra file, Fig5_GNFdesB.json, holds the third GNF triangle at 15 bar, which overlaps the other two by 3 px.
- Marker counts equal extracted counts in every archive. Figure 5 total: 82 markers identified and extracted (GNF 22, GNF-1000 20, EGNF-700 17, EGNF-1000 23).
- Calibration (gridline/frame centres found from pixel row/column profiles): Fig. 5 x 0 bar at px 714.4, 20 bar at 1155.6, check 10 bar at 935.5; y 0 at 506.2, 1.4 at 103.6, check 0.6 at 334.0. Fig. 6 x 0 at 175.3, 20 at 606.6, check 10 at 391; y 0 at 464.4, 0.3 at 103.46, check 0.1 at 344.36. Fig. 7 x 0 at 713.68, 20 at 1155.73, check 10 at 934.67; y -0.05 at 457.72, 0.3 at 103.41, check 0.1 at 306.0. All verification ticks within 0.3%.
- Step-6 checks: passed for Fig5_GNF_ads (0.34 at 20 bar, checked at 19.98 bar because the check needs a point inside the digitized range), Fig5_GNF1000_ads (0.18), Fig5_EGNF1000_ads (1.23), Fig6_GNF1000 (0.11), Fig6_EGNF700 (0.29), Fig7_EGNF150C (0.29), Fig7_E2GNF700 (0.03).
