## Method notes

- Digitization: `digitize_figure.py extract --mode markers` per series (colour mask, opening, connected-marker centroids), calibration from axis ticks with verify ticks, regions of interest set per panel to exclude the legend and spines. Blue triangles (UTL1/OP1) overlap red circles (UTL2/OP2) and dark squares (UTL3/OP3) in the crowded low-pressure region; blob exclusion and the <50 % visible filter are applied per series as listed above.
- Merge rule (§ on values that are both printed and plotted): a Table 5 / text value is merged with a plotted marker when it lies within 3 % (my reading of the criterion) of that marker's digitized value at the same pressure; otherwise it is a separate row. The 1 bar Table 5 values are kept separate for every sample except UTL1 because they do not coincide with the LP end markers and Table 5 gives no isotherm.
- Basis: excess uptake (Fig. 6a-d, Table 5 excess columns), total uptake (Fig. 6e/f, Table 5 total columns); wt% defined in Section 2.3 as m(H2)/(m_sample + m(H2)) (W2), stated. Temperature 77 K, isothermal, stated in captions. Pressure unit conversion mbar -> bar done in the rows (LP axis in mbar). Skeletal density used for Eq. 6 (1.9 g/cm3 in the paper, assumed value) not stored as a field.
- isotherm_point_count: LP isotherms 77 (57 adsorption + 20 desorption, Section 2.3 text). HP and total isotherms: estimated; the paper does not state the numbers.
- ACC: values taken from Table 6 are labelled previously reported ([4,28]); no sample description beyond Table 6 (BET 1318 m2/g, V_DFT 0.6 cm3/g, ZnCl2/CO2 activation).
- Out of scope for this run: nothing else in the paper reports hydrogen uptake of carbon sorbents.
