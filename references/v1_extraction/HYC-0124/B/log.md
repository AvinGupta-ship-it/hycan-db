## Digitization details and markers not separated

- Every digitized point is a tool marker centroid (digitize_figure.py extract --mode markers); archives are in digitizations/, one or more files per series (a series is split into several files where different regions or settings were needed).
- Empty (desorption) circles were isolated by their white interiors (colour #ffffff). Where the fit line splits an interior (or the 133 K MSP20X square fill) into fragments, the archive holds each fragment and the row uses the pixel-count-weighted mean of the fragment centroids converted with the archive calibration (MSC30 77 K desorption at 3.55, 5.07, 7.47, 10.91 MPa; MSP20X 133 K highest-pressure point). The 2.52 MPa MSC30 desorption interior lost a 1-px top sliver to the line (about 0.1 mmol/g low).

## Coding decisions worth a reviewer's look

- M48/M49/M53 (text maxima at 77 K, 4 MPa) are not merged with plotted points: no 77 K point lies within 2% / 0.02 bar of 40 bar (nearest adsorption points at about 3.0 and 5.07 MPa, desorption at 3.55 MPa), and the paper does not say the values were taken from the curve, so they carry no isotherm_id. M48 (29 mmol/g, 5.8 wt%) and M53 (28 mmol/g, 5.6 wt%) report the same MSC30 maximum with inconsistent numbers; both kept as printed.
- M53 temperature 77 K is taken from the Conclusion's statement of the same maximum; M52 (37 mmol/g absolute) has no printed temperature or pressure and is coded unstated (presumably 77 K).
- n_max rows (M50, M51, M54, M55): uptake_type unspecified, basis_inferred unknown (model saturation limit of the absolute amount; the volumetric excess default is not applied), measurement_mode left at isothermal for want of a better category. MSP20X rho_a: Table 2 prints 86.93 kg m-3, the text prints 87.74 kg m-3; Table 2 value recorded.
- bet_consistency_criteria_stated = true from "according to Rouquerol [47]" (ref. 47 is the Rouquerol BET-applicability paper); surface_area_probe_gas = N2 (N2 at 77 K is the textural isotherm named; BET range in P/P0). pore_diameter_method unspecified because Table 1 (DR and Stoeckli) and the text (NLDFT) disagree. calibration_described = false: only an empty-cell subtraction is described, not the free-volume determination. No H2 purity stated.
