## Method notes

- Digitization: scripts/digitize_figure.py extract --mode markers on page images in/pages (main-6, si1-10, si1-12). Axes calibrated on the lowest and highest labelled ticks and verified on a third tick (largest relative error of any verification tick 0.43 %). The legends of Fig 5 and S6 were masked in copies (work/imgs/*_nolegend.png) so legend symbols are not detected as data.
- Open markers: the tool cannot isolate an unfilled marker by its outline colour, so the white interior was detected (--color #ffffff), restricted to the plot region. White tolerance 8 (Fig S6 and Fig 5 ZTC8Pd-0.4, -0.8, -2.0), 100 (Fig 5 ZTC8 and ZTC8Pd-0.2) and 40 (Fig S8), opening 1 px (Fig 5, S8) or 2 px (Fig S6). The series was assigned by the colour of the ring around the hole and by position on a desorption grid column.
- Filled markers: series colour after a 3-px opening; only full-size detections (nominal area from the legend symbol in Fig S6; per-panel size range in Fig S8; core regions in Fig 5) are accepted, because a filled marker overlapped by an open marker leaves a fragment whose centroid is displaced.
- Archive structure: out/digitizations/<figure>_<sample>_<ads|des>.json are subsets of the tool outputs (the same calibration, isolation and checks fields, plus series subset with source_index, derived_from, curation, n_points; the rejected-marker lists are omitted). The complete unfiltered tool outputs they were derived from are in out/digitizations/raw/ (21 files, each covers several series). The `check` status (passed) was written by the tool into the derived archives after the final build; ads archives and 87 K archives have no stated value to check and are unchecked.
- Triangular markers: the tool returns the centroid of a triangle, which lies on the vertical axis of the marker but not at its geometric centre; no correction is applied and the resulting offset was not quantified.
- digitization_uncertainty_wt_pct is left empty as instructed; extraction_confidence is 5 for table values, 2 for Fig 5 digitized rows (small triangles, partly overlapping series) and 3 for Fig S6 and S8 digitized rows.
- Coding choices: uptake_type total for Table 1 and Fig 5 and excess for Table S1 and Fig S6, basis_stated = true because the paper names both bases and reports them side by side for the same samples; Fig S8 basis is not named (basis_stated = false, basis_inferred = excess). wt_pct_convention is unknown (the uptake density in umol m-2 is per BET area and is not a paired wt % convention). area_same_sample = yes, instrument_identified = false ("intelligent gravimetric analyzer (IGA)" is named without manufacturer or model), calibration_described = true (buoyancy correction), replicates_reported = true (repeat runs, Fig S7), measuring_lab_id = Mokaya group, University of Nottingham.
- Samples: S1 ZTC8, S2 ZTC8Pd-0.2, S3 ZTC8Pd-0.4, S4 ZTC8Pd-0.8, S5 ZTC8Pd-2.0, then the seven Table S2 ZTCs (S6-S12) and the ten Table S3 Pt-ZTCs (S13-S22). Isotherm ids: I1-I5 Fig 5 (total), I6-I10 Fig S6 (excess), I11-I14 Fig S8a (77 K), I15-I18 Fig S8b (87 K).
