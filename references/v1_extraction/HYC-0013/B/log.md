## Coding decisions
- basis_stated=false, basis_inferred=excess (volumetric Sieverts default), uptake_type=unspecified: the paper never names the basis of its uptake values.
- wt% convention: 77 K values printed with cm3(STP)/g. Read to the last printed digit (+/-0.5 cm3/g), 230->2.07 and 240->2.16 match W1 only (W1 inferred_from_paired_units); 60->0.54 matches both (unknown/none). If "230" and "240" are read as two significant figures, W2 also just matches and these would be unknown/none. 300 K and GO values are wt% only (unknown/none).
- instrument_identified=false (manufacturer, no model); calibration_described=false (skeletal density by He probing, free-volume determination not described); replicates_reported=false (Fig. 5a is called "representative"; no repeat results or uncertainties; Fig. 8b is a replot, not a repeat).
- measuring_lab_id "Hudson group, Central University of Tamil Nadu": Hudson is the marked corresponding author, affiliation a. The paper says "our apparatus" and does not say where the H2 measurements were made (co-authors at Banaras Hindu University and at Advanced Materials Corporation, the instrument maker).
- area_same_sample=yes: BET areas measured by the authors on the same four samples.
- GO has one uptake value and no plotted isotherm: no isotherm_id.
- extraction_confidence: 5 for Table 3 rows, 3 for digitized rows (150 dpi page, overlapping markers).
