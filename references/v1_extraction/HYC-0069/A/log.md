## Marker counts and digitization limits (Fig. 6a, 77 K)

- Open (desorption) markers are opaque squares with a white interior drawn over the closed (adsorption) markers of the same series, and the two carbon series overlap each other below about 2 bar. Counted by locating the enclosed white interiors of open markers (own image analysis, not the digitizer): SBL-CO-PP-C-7 27 open markers (0.97-19.7 bar), SBL-CO-PP-C-20 33 open markers (0.0-20.7 bar, the lowest two at 0.0 bar / 1.7 wt% and 0.8 bar / 1.55 wt%). Closed markers counted from the steps of the upper edge of the filled region: C-7 26 above about 2 bar, C-20 about 34 above about 2 bar. isotherm_point_count is therefore 53 (C-7) and 67 (C-20) as counted on the plot; markers below about 2 bar cannot be counted, so the true counts are higher. The count and the number of rows extracted differ for this reason.
- Open markers: not digitized. The digitizer's 3-px opening removes their 2-px outlines; with a smaller opening the outlines merge with the overlapping filled markers and the centroids are those of merged shapes. C-7 about 27 and C-20 about 33 desorption points are therefore not extracted.
- C-7 closed markers not extracted: the chain between about 2.5 and 13 bar is one 865-px blob of overlapping markers (rejected as too large); a 24-px remnant at 17.38 bar (rejected as too small, a marker mostly hidden behind an open one); two blobs below 1 bar (0.04 bar / 1.42 wt%, 32 px; 0.735 bar / 2.11 wt%, 78 px) where the magenta series covers the green one (excluded by starting the region at px 345). The 7 rows kept include markers partly hidden behind open markers: the centroid is that of the visible part and values can be up to about 0.07 wt% high (3 px); flagged in notes with extraction_confidence 2. The Table 2 value 4.4 vs digitized 4.37 wt% at 20.26 bar agrees to 0.8%.
- C-20 closed markers: none extracted. They are hidden behind the open markers; the digitizer returns 7 blobs (1.34 bar / 1.80 wt%, 105 px; 7.66 / 3.19, 36 px; 9.18 / 3.33, 36 px; 10.38 / 3.44, 84 px; 13.35 / 3.64, 36 px; 16.81 / 3.85, 32 px; 17.67 / 3.92, 42 px) and rejects 16 more (15 of 9-27 px, the last at 21.13 bar / 4.09 wt%; one 606-px blob at the origin). Where checked the blob is a strip about 3 px wide on the left edge of a hidden marker, with its centroid 4-5 px (0.3-0.4 bar) left of the marker centre, so they are not centres of markers and no row is written. The tool output is kept in the work directory (HYC-0069_fig6a_SBL-CO-PP-C-20_77K_unused_slivers.json, status unchecked). Consequence: the Table 2 row M11 (4.1 wt%, 20 bar) has no digitized point merged into it; the nearest plotted points are an open marker at 19.92 bar (about 4.02 wt%, desorption) and a hidden closed marker, so the "same measurement" test could not be completed by the digitizer.
