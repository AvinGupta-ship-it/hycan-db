## Coding decisions on ambiguous points
- wt% convention W1 / stated, from the Section 2.2 definition "wt%—weight percent of adsorbed gas per gram of adsorbent". Basis not named by the paper: basis_stated false, uptake_type unspecified, basis_inferred excess (volumetric default).
- measurement_method volumetric_sieverts for both runs. The 80 bar run is "a Sieverts' type apparatus (fPcT) [46]" (instrument_identified false). The 1 bar run is the Micromeritics ASAP 2460 (instrument_identified true); the paper does not name its technique, I code the ASAP 2460 as a static volumetric analyzer.
- calibration_described false (no free-volume or buoyancy statement). Degassing before H2 "≈473 K overnight": 199.85 C stored, duration not numeric, no end pressure. No sample mass or H2 purity stated.
- replicates_reported true on rows whose value or isotherm carries error bars (Figures 8 and 10), Table 2 ± values, or repeated runs (Figure S5). The 0.38 wt% text row is false. Figure 9 caption says the error "is the size of the symbol itself".
- Surface area: BET, probe gas N2 (named for the N2 isotherms at 77 K), range 0.05–0.15 stated, no consistency criteria, area_same_sample yes (same samples and 473 K degassing). V_T pressure is P/P0 0.99 in the Table 1 footnote and 0.995 in Section 2.2. Pore volumes are DFT on N2; the ultramicropore cutoff is 7 Å (0.7 nm) from the footnote; the super-micropore column is the 7–20 Å increment (ultra + super = V_micro), not a field.
- average_pore_diameter_nm 0.5 for the three activated samples is the approximate PSD peak ("centered around 0.5 nm", Section 3.2 and Conclusions); not given for DPC (Figure S2 shows a different DPC peak).
- Measuring lab: corresponding author A. Policicchio (first affiliation Dipartimento di Fisica, Università della Calabria).
- Figure legends print "ADPC0,5"; Table 1 prints "ADPC0.5" and is used as the label.
- Observation only: Table 2 ADPC3 wt%max (4.980) is far above the Figure 9 ADPC3 plateau (about 3.4) while ADPC1 (5.435) and ADPC0.5 (1.634) are close to theirs, which would fit a fit to the 0–1 bar data; the paper does not say, so Table 2 rows carry no isotherm_id.
