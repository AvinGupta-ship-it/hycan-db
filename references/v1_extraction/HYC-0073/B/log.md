## Fig. 3 digitization

- Calibration on main-4.png (page pixels), ticks located by pixel rows/columns: x 0 bar = 227.5 (tick on the y-axis line, cols 227-228), 20 bar = 500.5 (right frame, cols 500-501), check 10 bar = 364 (error 0); y 0 wt% = row 382 (x-axis line), 6 wt% = row 130, check 4 wt% = row 214 (error 0). Other ticks consistent (5 bar 295.75, 15 bar 432; 5 wt% 172, 3 wt% 255.5, 2 wt% 297.6). The 1 wt% tick is partly hidden by markers.
- A whole-plot colour run cannot isolate markers (greyscale figure; hollow black markers joined by black/grey lines; 3-px opening deletes hollow markers, no opening fragments them). Each marker was therefore run separately: scripts/digitize_figure.py extract --mode markers, colour #000000, tolerance 225, --open-px 0, a 9x9 px ROI centred on the marker (ROI centres located from the enclosed white interior of each hollow marker). Each run returned exactly one marker (Zr-CDC marker 1 needed --marker-min-px 6 to drop a 4-px fragment of the neighbouring marker). Per-series files in digitizations/ bundle the per-marker run archives verbatim (per_marker_runs) with the points in series[].
- Sanity check (digitize_figure.py check, 5%) against Table 1 at the 20 bar end point: Zr-CDC 3.895 vs 3.8 (+2.5%, pass), AC-600 4.874 vs 4.9 (-0.5%, pass), AC-800 5.757 vs 5.8 (-0.7%, pass). The check was run at the digitized end-point pressure (19.950, 19.976, 19.979 bar) because the tool refuses x outside the digitized range.
- Marker counts in the separable range (counted on the plot = extracted): Zr-CDC 19 markers from 1.5 to 20 bar (0.5-bar steps to 4 bar, 1-bar steps to 10 bar, then 11.5, 13, 14.5, 16, 17.5, 19, 20 bar); AC-600 19 markers from 2 to 20 bar (1-bar steps); AC-800 18 markers from 3 to 20 bar (1-bar steps).
- 20 bar end points: merged with the Table 1 20 bar values (printed pressure within 2% of the digitized end point); recorded as table_direct rows with isotherm_id and "digitized:" notes; no separate digitized row.

## Plotted points not extracted (could not be separated)

- AC-700 (open squares) and AC-900 (stars), whole isotherms: at every pressure the AC-900 star sits on the upper edge of the AC-700 square (centres ca. 4 px, 0.1 wt%, apart; markers ca. 7 px), so each pair is one connected ink blob and the tool returns one merged centroid or fragments; no marker of either series was digitized. Their Table 1 values (20 and 1 bar) are rows without isotherm_id.
- Low-pressure markers of all five isotherms: below ca. 1.5 bar (Zr-CDC), 2 bar (AC-600) and 3 bar (AC-800), and for AC-700/AC-900 everywhere, the markers of the five series overlap into one blob along the y axis (0-3 wt%); they cannot be separated or counted. This includes the plotted points near 1 bar, so the Table 1 values at 1 bar could not be matched to a plotted point and carry no isotherm_id.
- Desorption branches: the paper states the isotherms are reversible with no hysteresis; the desorption markers are plotted on top of the adsorption markers (same pressures). For Zr-CDC the two branches are visibly two lines offset by up to ca. 0.1 wt% with partly overlapping markers. The tool cannot separate the two markers of a pair, so each digitized point is the centroid of the visible (coincident) marker, coded adsorption; no desorption rows.
- isotherm_point_count is left empty on I1-I3 because the total number of markers (both branches, including the overlapping low-pressure region) cannot be counted.
