## Digitization method and checks
- Every archive in digitizations/ was made by `digitize_figure.py extract --mode markers` on a DERIVED image (work/derived/<panel>_<series>.png), not on the page PNG itself: the panel crop was enlarged 4x (bicubic), the series colour was isolated by unmixing against its legend colour, connecting lines were removed by morphological opening, and the interiors of open-marker rings were kept as blobs. Calibration ticks were located on the original page image (lowest and highest labelled ticks, third labelled tick as residual check, all within 0.4%) and converted exactly into derived-image coordinates; every value comes from the tool run on the derived image. The archive `source_image` is therefore the derived PNG.
- Marker centres are connected-component centroids; for triangles the area centroid may sit up to about 1 page pixel from the symbol centre (about 0.02 wt% on Fig. 4d).
- Marker semantics: the paper does not say what open and filled markers mean (Sec. 3.3 only says "Reversible H2 absorption isotherms ... are observed without any hysteresis (Figure 4a,b)"). Per the rule that a branch is adsorption unless the paper says desorption, all rows are `adsorption`; the marker type is recorded in `notes` (open presumed desorption by convention). Filled and open markers largely alternate in pressure along Fig. 4d (filled at one set of pressures, open at the others), consistent with that convention.
- Fig. 4d legend lists NHPC-2 as red triangles-down and NHPC-3 as pink triangles-up, the reverse of Fig. 4a-c and 4e; the series were assigned by colour as in the other panels (pink = NHPC-2, red = NHPC-3), which also agrees with the order of the curves and with Fig. 4e.
- Checks run with `digitize_figure.py check` (printed value of Table 3 or text, at a plotted pressure inside the series range): PASS Fig4a NHPC-2 (3.02 at 100.1 kPa) and NHPC-3 (2.08 at 100.27 kPa); Fig4b NHPC-1, -2, -3 (2.02, 2.10, 1.46); Fig4c NHPC-1 (0.05, tolerance-abs 0.005 = printed rounding), NHPC-2, NHPC-3 (0.09, 0.09); Fig4e NHPC-0 (6.10 at 49.70 bar, digitized 6.03), NHPC-1 (5.40, digitized 5.548, 2.7%), NHPC-2 (4.99, digitized 4.92), NHPC-3 (3.16); Fig5c NHPC-1, -2, -3 (28.35, 26.96, 18.61 g/L). That tolerance (+-0.0025) is narrower than the printed rounding of "0.05" (+-0.005), so it was a wrong test, not a bad reading; the series was re-extracted unchanged (same calibration and parameters, same 23 markers) and re-checked with an absolute tolerance of 0.005 (PASS).
