## Figure digitization method

- Axes calibrated on the lowest and highest labelled ticks with a third tick as check (tick pixel positions found from pixel rows and columns): 8a x 0 bar @293.0, 100 @589.0, check 60 @471.0; y 0 @377.0, 7 @147.0, check 4 @245.5. 8b x 0 @677.5, 100 @965.5, check 60 @850.5; y 0 @371.0, 35 @153.0, check 20 @246.5. 9a x 0 @310.0, 100 @599.0, check 60 @483.0; y 0 @388.0, 2.5 @134.0, check 1.0 @286.5. 9b x 0 @676.0, 100 @972.5, check 60 @854.0; y 0 @389.0, 7 @152.5, check 4 @253.5. All checks within 0.3%.
- Legends were painted white in work copies of the page images (work/p09_f8a_masked.png, p09_f8b_masked.png, p10_f9_masked.png); pixel coordinates unchanged.
- Markers could not be separated: in every Figure 8 and 9 series the markers (about 7-9 px) are spaced about 3 px (about 1.0 bar, from the periodicity of the band edge), so they merge into a continuous band. Marker mode returned 4 merged blobs for the 8a DACA series (trial archive kept in work/trial/). No marker of any series could be separated or counted, so isotherm_point_count is empty on all rows (about 100 points per isotherm is implied by the 1 bar spacing; not recorded).
- Each band was therefore read with the line procedure: curve mode, then linear interpolation of the trace at k/10 x p_end (k = 1-10). p_end is the band's right end minus the marker radius (half the band thickness between 30 and 60 bar), i.e. the centre of the last marker; it is 99.8-100.0 bar for every series, consistent with the stated 0-100 bar range. Figure 8b was read at the pressures of the Figure 8a grid (its own p_end agrees within 0.35 bar). Digitized rows are value_kind = fitted_interpolated because the paper does not state that a line joins measured points.
- Hot-15tDACA (light purple) used tolerance 40 and a region excluding the upper part of the plot (8a x>=300, y>=290; 8b x>=692, y>=290), because at tolerance 60 grey text anti-aliasing and the anti-aliased edges of the in situDIW (purple) band matched its colour.
- Overlapping series: rows whose band is partly hidden at the read pressure carry a note and confidence 2 (M9 DACA 8b at 90 bar, hidden by Cold-5tDACA; M38-M39 Cold-5tDACA 8a at 80-90 bar, hidden by DIW; M41 Hot-5tDACA at 10 bar, almost fully hidden under Hot-10tDACA in both panels, reading follows the lower edge and is likely low by up to about 3 px, about 0.1 wt% and 0.5 g/L). The 100 bar points of DACA (8b) and Cold-5tDACA (8a) are also partly hidden; those rows hold the printed values and the digitized values are in notes.
- Digitizer check (step 6) run only where the paper states both pressure and value (in situDIW at 100 bar: 8a 5.9 wt%, 8b 36 g/L, 9a 1.8 wt%, 9b 6.9 g/L): all passed at 5%. Other archives remain "unchecked" because no pressure is printed with their values.
- Figure 9b legend labels the orange series "DIW" (misprint; the navy series is also "DIW"). The orange series was assigned to DACA by colour (orange = DACA in Figures 7-10 and 9a) and by the printed 5.0 g/L.

## Coding decisions

- Basis: "total" is named and defined (SI eq. S1, G_tot = G_ex + rho_gas*V_a) and excess is reported separately (Figure 10a), so basis_stated = true, basis_inferred = total. SI eq. S5 defines "absolute" uptake by the same expression; noted on every row.
- Degassing before H2: main text 120 C for 12 h (recorded); SI gives "10 h at their respective activation temperatures" and "150 C for 12 hours ... approximately 6.7 x 10^-2 Pa" (end pressure recorded). Conflict noted on every row.
- instrument_identified = false: SI names BELSORP-HP (model) without a manufacturer. calibration_described = true (helium expansion). replicates_reported = true for 298 K ("measured at least in duplicate, with deviations below 5%"), false for 77 K.
- Table 1 V<0.7 recorded as ultramicropore volume (cutoff 0.7 nm, NLDFT); its footnote calls it V_micro. V0.7-25 (footnote: V_meso) not recorded as mesopore volume because its 0.7-25 nm range includes supermicropores. micropore_volume left empty.
- bet_range_stated = false: the only relative pressure given ("at a relative pressure of P/P0 = 0.9") is not a BET fitting range.
- Sample numbering by first appearance (Abstract: in situDIW, then DACA as the "powder counterpart"; Introduction: DIW; Section 2.2: hot pressing at 5, 10, 15 tons, then cold pressing): S1 in situDIW, S2 DACA, S3 DIW, S4 Hot-5tDACA, S5 Hot-10tDACA, S6 Hot-15tDACA, S7 Cold-5tDACA.
- Pellet bulk densities are given only as a range (0.5-0.55 g/cm3); packing_density recorded only for DACA (0.21), DIW (0.45) and in situDIW (0.52).
