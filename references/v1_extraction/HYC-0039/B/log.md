## Decisions

Labels as printed in Table 2.
- Basis: Section 2 names and defines excess (Gibbs) -> basis_stated true, basis_inferred excess on all rows.
- Surface area: the paper prints no surface-area number for any sample (only "ammonia treatment improved the BET surface area") -> surface_area_method none on all rows; area-dependent codes left empty.
- calibration_described true: "The adsorbent specimen volume was corrected on the basis of skeletal density measurements, corresponding to the excess values obtained by helium gas" (free-volume determination by He).
- replicates_reported true on every row: Table 2 gives +/-0.1 wt% (77 K) and +/-0.01 wt% (298 K) for each isotherm; uncertainty_wt_pct stored only on the Table 2 rows. The statement that PCI curves were measured several times gives no results and is not the basis.
- Fig 10b legend: the four series use the same symbols and line colours as Fig 10a (red squares/black line, yellow diamonds/red line, green stars/green line, blue diamonds/blue line) but the legend text is permuted. Assigned by the Fig 10a key, which agrees with Table 2 (AC 0.21, Pt-AC 0.26, NH3-AC 0.33, Pt-NH3-AC 0.37 vs plotted end values 0.207, 0.258, 0.348, 0.371) and with the text; the Fig 10b legend text would contradict Table 2 for all four. Table rows for these four carry extraction_confidence 4 and a note.
- Branches: each figure plots adsorption and desorption with the same symbol and line colour, with no marking of which line is which. All digitized points are coded isotherm_branch adsorption by the default rule, with a note.
- Partly hidden markers: a marker partly covered by another marker or clipped by the frame is digitized only when at least about half of it is visible (tool pixel count >= ~50% of a clean marker of the same series); otherwise it is counted but not digitized. A marker cut into fragments by an overlying line is recorded at the pixel-weighted centroid of the tool fragments. Two overlapping same-colour markers are separated only when an axis-aligned region split is clean (targeted --roi runs, archives *__t<n>.json); otherwise they are counted, not digitized.
- isotherm_point_count: counted on the plot including hidden markers; where a band of overlapping markers prevents an exact count (Figs 11a-15a at about 3-16 bar; low-pressure parts of all 298 K panels) the stored count is a lower bound, noted on the rows.
- Axis calibration: lowest and highest labelled ticks, third labelled tick verified in every archive (relative error <= 0.32%). Points above the highest or below the lowest labelled tick are noted as such (linear axes).
- digitize_figure.py check: Fig 10a, all four series at 7 bar vs Table 2: pass (0.03-1.2%). Fig 11b Pd-AC at 100 bar vs 0.41: pass (3.9%). Fig 14b Ir-AC at 100 bar vs 0.43: pass (2.9%). Fig 14b Ir-NH3-AC could not be checked at 100 bar (main archive range ends at 99.2 bar because of a merged end pair). Figs 11a-15a not checked at 7 bar: the series there are interrupted by unseparable bands.
- Merges at 7 bar (77 K): Fig 10a points closest to 7 bar (6.2-6.3 bar) merged with Table 2. Figs 11a-15a: the plotted points near 7 bar lie in the overlapping band, so the Table 2 row has no digitized point (noted).
