## Coding decisions worth a second look

- measuring_lab_id: the three corresponding authors are marked * (Attia), ** (Lee), *** (Oh). I read "the first one marked" as first in the author list (Lee, Kyungpook National University). Reading it as the first mark (Attia, GNTECH) is also possible; this is ambiguous and the value is the same on every row.
- Basis: Table 4 reports excess and total side by side (basis_stated = true, quote in basis_evidence) for I1 to I10 and the total rows. The 2.7 wt% row, the 126.6 cm3/g row and Fig S3 (I11) name no basis for their own value; they are basis_stated = false, basis_inferred = excess by the Sieverts default (the Sec. 3.3 sentence ties Table S1 and Fig S3 to "excess" uptake, so I11 has uptake_type = excess; the other two are unspecified).
- Material class: doped_carbon for all five samples, because the paper reports sulphur functional groups by FTIR in all of them and calls the sulphur "natural doping". dopant_concentration_at_pct is the S content of Table 1 (EDS); M-C-4-700 has no EDS row, so it is empty. K and Ca residues in M-C-800 and Ca in M-C-APS-4-800 are recorded as residual_metal_element only (Table 1 gives at%, not wt%).
- pore_volume_method = DR (micropore volume) with N2; the total pore volume is by DFT, and the mesopore volume is Vtot - Vmicro (stated in notes). pore_diameter_method = DFT (QSDFT slit pore, adsorption branch).
- replicates_reported = true for M-C-KOH-4-800 rows at 77 K only: two H2 runs of that sample (1st and Last, Fig S3) are shown. The 3 replicate syntheses (carbon yield within 2%) are not H2 results.
- calibration_described = false: the free-volume or total-uptake method is only referred to ref. 28, not described. instrument_identified = true (Setaram PCTPro-E&E).
- Digitized rows use extraction_confidence 3; digitization_uncertainty_wt_pct is empty as instructed. Pressures are in bar as plotted; the paper does not say gauge or absolute.
- Archives with status discarded (Fig7b_M-C-4-800_298K, Fig7b_M-C-800_298K) or unchecked (FigS3_Last) stay in digitizations/ for the record and produce no rows.
