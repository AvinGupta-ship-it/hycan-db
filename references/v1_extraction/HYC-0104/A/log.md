## Digitization notes

- Page images are 150 dpi, so a marker is 6-7 px across and 1 px = 0.04-0.05 MPa on the pressure axes and 0.012-0.013 wt% on the uptake axes. Pressures were read in MPa and multiplied by 10 for bar.
- Calibration (tick pixels found by inspecting pixel rows and columns; lowest and highest labelled ticks used, a middle tick as check; tool check errors are in each archive): Fig. 9a x 0 -> px 255, 14 -> px 591, check 6; y 0 -> px 421, 3 -> px 165, check 1.5. Fig. 9c x 0 -> 268, 14 -> 615, check 6; y 0 -> 738, 3 -> 520, check 1.5. Fig. 9d x 0 -> 702, 14 -> 1019, check 6; y 0 -> 738, 3 -> 504, check 2. SI Fig. S7 (si1-06.png) x 0 -> 419, 18 -> 858, check 8; y 0 -> 528, 3 -> 254, check 2. All checks within 0.4%.
- The tool could not isolate markers directly from the page images, because the thick red fit line is drawn over the markers (splitting them) and dashed or connecting lines touch them. Each series was therefore run on a derived image with the same pixel grid as the page (so calibration is unchanged), made by `work/isolate.py`: pixels within colour distance 150 of the series colour are kept in pure series colour on white, a 3x3 closing bridges the 1-2 px gap left by the fit line (not used for Fig. 9a, where filled markers are separable), legend items and text are blanked, and the ROI is limited to the plot. The derived images are in `work/iso/`; each archive records the derived image path and sha256 as `source_image`.
- SI Fig. S7: two triangles (P ~0.4 and ~0.6 MPa) are in contact and formed one blob; 3 px (x 429-431, y 334 in si1-06.png, the apex row of the lower triangle directly under the base of the upper one) were blanked so the tool separated them. 20 markers counted, 20 extracted.
- Main Fig. 9b (same TE7 data as S7) was run as a cross-check only (not archived): the tool found 18 components, because three triangles at 0.3-0.7 MPa merge into one blob; the 17 separable markers agree with S7 within 0.7% in pressure and 0.5% in uptake. This is why TE7 I1 is taken from S7.
- Rejected by the tool as too small (not markers): Fig. 9d one dashed-line fragment at px (702, 697); Fig. 9a TE7 one partly hidden triangle at px (264, 252), P ~0.37 MPa, ~1.98 wt%.
- Markers whose centre lies at or below the axis zero were digitized but not recorded as rows, because pressure cannot be resolved from zero at this resolution (the validator rejects pressure 0): I1 marker 1 (x -0.022 MPa, 0.147 wt%); I3 markers 1-2 (x 0.0, 0.846 and 0.206 wt%); I4 markers 1-3 (x -0.016, 0.0, 0.0; -0.002, 0.369, 0.158 wt%); I5 markers 1-2 (x -0.022, -0.022; 0.248, -0.005 wt%). They are included in `isotherm_point_count`. Numbers are in the archives.
- Fig. 9a is crowded below 0.3 MPa (markers of all three samples stacked at the same pressure). Filled markers of TE7 below 0.6 MPa are hidden by black squares and open symbols and were not extracted (I2 is therefore a lower bound of 12). The TiC filled marker at the origin overlaps markers of the other two samples.
- Fig. 9a open symbols (squares, triangles, circles with centre dot) are not extracted for any sample: they are 1-px outlines crossed by connecting lines, overlap filled markers, and are stacked below 0.3 MPa, so the tool (3 px opening) cannot return centres. The caption of Fig. 9 does not define the open symbols; by the filled/open convention of Fig. 3 and the Methods ("adsorption/desorption measurements", "fully reversible") they are the desorption branch. They are not counted in `isotherm_point_count`, which is a lower bound for I2, I4 and I5.
- Fig. 9a vs the single-sample panels: the filled blue circles coincide with Fig. 9d (13 markers compared: pressure within 1%, uptake within 1%), so SWCNT Fig. 9a is the same run as I5 and is not repeated. TE7 and TiC-CDC-800 filled markers of Fig. 9a only partly coincide with Fig. 9b/S7 and 9c/S5 (different pressure grids, e.g. TE7 0.87, 1.18, 1.91, 2.37, 2.80, 3.66, 10.84 MPa have no counterpart; TiC has 22 markers against 17 and different high-pressure points), so they are recorded as separate plotted curves (I2, I4), as the instructions say each plotted curve is one run.
- The `check` sub-command of the tool was not run: no uptake value at a stated pressure is printed anywhere in the paper to compare with, so every archive stays `unchecked`; only the third-tick calibration check was done.
