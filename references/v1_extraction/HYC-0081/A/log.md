Digitization record

- Archives in `digitizations/` (8 files). Axis calibrations use the lowest and highest labelled ticks plus a middle labelled tick as check (residuals in each archive: all under 0.35%). Tick pixel positions were found from pixel rows/columns of the page images.
- Figures 3d, 4a (77 K) and S12 (77 K): the markers overlap into a solid band (Fig. 3d, filled triangles) or a dense chain of open triangles (Fig. 4a, S12). Individual markers cannot be separated, so `--mode markers` returns one blob; `--mode curve` was used and read at k/10 x p_end (k = 1-10) by linear interpolation. p_end is the apex of the last marker (Fig. 3d 0.9923 atm; Fig. 4a 0.9886 atm; Fig. S12 0.9913 atm). Rows are `fitted_interpolated` (the paper does not state that the line joins measured points), except the two text rows. `isotherm_point_count` left empty for these isotherms because the markers cannot be counted (a rough pitch estimate from the sawtooth edge suggested of the order of 50 markers, not used). Bin width: 1 px (Fig. 3d), 4 px (Fig. 4a), 6 px (Fig. S12), chosen to match the marker pitch of the open chains.
- Fig. 3d PGF-1 (red) readings k = 1-3 (P < 0.35 atm): the lower part of the red band is hidden under the blue band, so the median is biased high (about 10% at k = 1, 2.5% at k = 2); the Fig. 4a PGF-1 reading at 0.099 atm (59.9 cc/g) is the cleaner value. Confidence 2 on these three rows.
- Fig. 4a: the blue "77K" label overlies bins at x = 274-289 px (flagged as multimodal bins in the archive; effect about 1% at k = 7). Fig. S12: the label lies above the chain.
- 87 K points (Fig. 4a, S12): centroid of the last open triangle (markers mode, ROI around the last marker, 1-px opening). The previous marker overlaps its left side slightly, so the centroid may be shifted a little. No stated value to check against; archives left `unchecked`.

Coding decisions worth a second look

- Sample numbering: only samples with H2 rows are numbered (S1 = PGF-1, S2 = PGF-2). RGO, RGO-IBz and GO have no H2 value, so no sample id.
- `material_class` = reduced_graphene_oxide (RGO covalently pillared by hydrocarbon linkers, described by the authors as all-carbon); `synthesis_method` = chemical_reduction (NaBH4 reduction of Hummers graphite oxide); pillaring is not a synthesis route for this field. No dopant or metal recorded (no Pd/Cu/I content reported).
- Basis not named: `uptake_type` = unspecified, `basis_stated` = false, `basis_inferred` = excess by the volumetric default (Quadrasorb SI and BELSORP-HP are volumetric). 87 K rows: instrument and sample preparation not stated for 87 K, coded `measurement_method` = unknown, `basis_inferred` = unknown, `instrument_identified` = false, degassing fields empty.
- Quadrasorb passage (SI p. 3-4) describes helium being expanded from the gas chamber into the sample chamber and Pcal - Pe, but does not say it gives the free volume; `calibration_described` = false for low-pressure rows. BELSORP-HP: dead volume measured with He, so true.
- Degassing for low-pressure rows from SI (433 K, about 6 h, 1e-1 Pa); the main text gives 160 C for 12 h for the N2 measurements. Sample mass for low-pressure rows is a range (about 100-150 mg), not stored; high-pressure "approximately 200 mg" stored as 200.
- `measuring_lab_id`: corresponding authors are Maji (first marked) and Rao; Maji's only affiliation (a) names no university, so the institute is used: "International Centre for Material Science". The paper does not say another laboratory measured H2.
- `wt_pct_convention`: unknown/none for printed wt% (text, Fig. 4b); the cc/g and mL/g curve values give W1 computed with 0.0089938 (source computed_from_other_unit). The printed 1.2 wt% could be either convention (135.4 cc/g gives 1.2175 W1 or 1.2027 W2).
- `isotherm_id`: none for the two 87 K points (the only value for that sample and temperature). `pore volume 0.74 cm3/g` ("estimated pore volume" from the NL-DFT fit of the N2 isotherm) stored as total pore volume; the paper does not say total.
- Replicates: none reported (no uncertainty, no repeated isotherm result). `replicates_reported` = false.
