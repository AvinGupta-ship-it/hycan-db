## Digitization (Fig. 5 and Fig. 6)

- Fig. 5 calibration (page main-06): x 0 mbar at px 291.33, 1200 mbar at px 944.33, check 600 mbar at px 617.67 (0.05% error); y 0 mmol/g at px 1079.0, 16 at px 679.68, check 8 at px 879.32 (0.01%). Tick positions from pixel-column/row darkness centroids. Pressure stored in bar (mbar/1000). Values read on the left axis (V Ads, mmol/g); uptake_wt_pct is W1 computed from them. The right axis (H2 storage, wt%) is a fixed 0.2 x mmol/g rescaling (16 mmol/g = 3.2 wt%) and was not read separately.
- Fig. 6 calibration (page main-07): x 0 bar at px 286.0, 100 bar at px 931.0, check 40 bar at px 544.0 (0.0%); y 0 wt% at px 510.5 (axis line rows 510-511), 0.6 at px 105.67, check 0.3 at px 308.0 (0.04%). y axis is wt% only, so uptake_wt_pct is the digitized value (convention unknown).
- All Fig. 5 series are black or grey on white, so they cannot be isolated by colour. For each series I wrote a derived image (same size and pixel coordinates as the page image, kept in the work directory as img_fig5_<series>.png) that keeps the page pixels only inside that series' marker windows: a band of 1.0-4.5 px half-width around the series' polyline, split at each marker (markers found as peaks of dark-pixel width across the band, and by explicit x intervals in the touching clusters near 1070-1110 and 1160-1180 mbar), with a 1-2 px gap between neighbouring windows. For open markers (circles, squares, triangles, diamonds) enclosed holes of <= 30 px were filled black so that a cut ring stays one blob. digitize_figure.py extract --mode markers then gave the centroid of each blob (colour #000000, tolerance 260, i.e. grey < ~150; opening 1 px, 2 px for MAXSORB, 3 px for PRA50). In every final run the number of extracted markers equals the number of windows, which equals the markers I counted in the digitized range.
- Isolated low-pressure markers were added from tight boxes: MAXSORB filled diamonds at 143-357 mbar (7, cores after opening), ACC20 circles at 240, 294, 372 mbar, ACC10 triangles at 244, 297, 372, 409, 419 mbar, WKL20 square at 297 mbar, MWNT/MWNT2 markers at 150-300 mbar.
- Digitized per series (77 K): MAXSORB 37 (0.144-1.198 bar), PER50 28 (0.450-1.199), PRA50 28 (0.450-1.199), PICA 28 (0.449-1.199), WKL20 32 (0.297-1.199), ACC20 33 (0.240-1.198), ACC15 31 (0.369-1.199), ACC10 33 (0.244-1.199), SWNT 45 (0.019-1.199), MWNT 37 (0.149-1.198), MWNT2 35 (0.151-1.199). Only adsorption branches are plotted.

  - PICA, PRA50 and PER50 at about 372, 400 and 418 mbar: one merged blob per pressure (three series superimposed);
  - WKL20 at about 240-290 mbar and below: merged with PICA/PRA50/PER50 markers;
  - ACC15 below about 370 mbar: plus signs overlap ACC20 circles, MAXSORB diamonds and ACC10 triangles (e.g. near 294 and 218 mbar);
