## Coding decisions worth a second look

- Basis: "Excess" is named in Fig. 7a axis, Table 4 header, abstract and section 3.4 but never defined and no second basis is given: basis_stated = false, uptake_type = excess, basis_inferred = excess (named without definition); the naming quote is kept in basis_evidence. Applied to both temperatures (abstract and section 3.4 call the 298 K isotherms excess).
- wt% convention: not defined, no paired units: unknown / none.
- material_class doped_carbon: N is built in via polypyrrole (12.93 to 7.85 at.%); the residual Zn (<= 0.78 at.%) and Cl come from ZnCl2/NH4Cl impregnation and are not treated as a metal phase. synthesis_method carbonization (raw carbon made by carbonizing VRC-PNP; viscose cloth is a commercial precursor but the sorbent is not purchased as such, so material_identity is empty). Activation is in activation_method.
- sample_mass_mg 100 from "~100 mg of each sample was degassed"; stored as printed digits, approximate. h2_purity_pct 99.999 from "99.999% purity" in the H2 measurement sentence (could be read as the purity of H2, CH4 and CO2); calibration_described false (no free-volume statement), replicates_reported false (adsorption/desorption branches are not replicates; the cycling of Fig. S5 is CH4/CO2).
- measuring_lab_id: corresponding author H. Oh (marked *), first affiliation Gyeongnam National University of Science and Technology; the paper does not name another laboratory for the H2 measurements.
- Fig. 7a desorption is plotted for 298 K in the caption but Fig. 7b shows closed symbols only; no 298 K desorption rows.
- Merge rule applied literally (printed 20 bar, tolerance max(2%, 0.02 bar) = 0.4 bar, adsorption markers only, nearest in pressure when several qualify, step 6 check must pass). Cases where the literal rule and the value agreement differ: C30 77 K (20.408 bar, 0.008 bar outside, value 3.596 vs printed 3.60); C60 77 K (printed 4.00 vs digitized 3.92); C85 77 K (nearest markers 0.54 and 0.60 bar away, value 2.844 vs 2.83); P-CC-PNP 77 K merged with the 19.789 bar marker (21 px, partly covered) rather than the 20.295 bar marker (2.417 wt%).
